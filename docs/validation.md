- Electron UI checks: 58 passed using an isolated application-data directory and a deterministic YouTube player double. Covered loading without autoplay, play/pause animation state, seeking, volume/mute, playlist next/previous, mode switching without player recreation, minimum player dimensions, native resizing, unavailable-video errors, Node.js isolation, and restart persistence. Also covered native YouTube audio synchronization, preserving volume while muted, immediate mute persistence, retrying failed API downloads, ignoring stale callbacks from replaced players, and waiting for the actual cue before enabling Play. - Home toggle checks verify the enlarged room, hidden video panel, normal-mode restoration, the same player instance and time, no extra cue/play/pause commands, and preserving a deliberate pause. Responsive checks enforce a large 3:2 room in Home at every tested size and 16:9 video in normal mode.
- Background regressions cover another native window fully covering Haru.fm, a hidden-visibility notification, minimization via the app button and native window controls, continuing renderer/player time, playlist advancement without replacing the iframe, continued playback on restore, and preserving a deliberate pause. System suspend is separate: it still pauses even while minimized, rejects a playing event during sleep, and resume does not autoplay. Play works again after restore/wake. The clock is a player double, so these checks do not prove real streamed audio.
- Linux headless verification checks the requested pin preference and constructs the cover window. Its compositor has no stacking order or taskbar state; the Windows workflow additionally verifies the real native always-on-top flag, full coverage by a visible topmost window, and minimized state.
- Responsive-window checks: 22 passed in Electron at 125% display scaling. Ten native window sizes in both modes cover the 420×540 minimum, tall/narrow and wide/short shapes, long multilingual titles, visible window/player/transport/link controls, no document scrolling, 16:9 video, 3:2 artwork, and playback without player recreation. Both modes also fit an error notice at the minimum size. These checks run as part of `npm run check` on Windows.
- Queue and playlist checks: 18 passed locally. Cover adding without autoplay, Play queue, current-relative Play next, automatic advance, no wrap at queue end, preserving current identity through reorder, save/rename, append/replace, unavailable-track skip, removing current song, minimum expanded geometry without remounting, Mini panel behavior, rejecting full YouTube playlist imports, persistence without autoplay, clear without deleting saved playlists, and explicit deletion confirmation, clearing during a pending cue, and removing a paused current track without autoplay. ## Windows acceptance

After downloading or running the app on Windows:

1. Paste an embeddable YouTube video link, click Load, then Play. Switch to normal video mode with Home if needed. Confirm visible video and audible sound.
2. Pause, seek, change volume, mute and unmute. Change volume/mute inside YouTube and confirm Haru.fm follows; confirm custom Unmute restores sound.
3. Load a YouTube playlist and use Next/Previous. Add three individual videos to the Haru queue, play it, reorder them, use Play next, and let one finish to verify automatic next. Save and rename a playlist, append it, replace the queue, close/reopen and confirm persistence without autoplay. Open the queue at a small size in both modes and confirm the active visual area and controls remain unobstructed.
4. Press Home while playing. Confirm the room becomes large and the video hides; press it again and confirm the normal video returns at the same playback position. Pause deliberately and toggle twice to confirm it stays paused.
5. Pin the app, switch to another application, and confirm Haru.fm remains above it. Drag the title bar and resize the window in both modes. Confirm the video and artwork keep their proportions, controls and link field remain visible, and the page does not scroll.
6. Unpin the app and cover it completely with another window. Confirm audio continues. Minimize using the app button and Windows taskbar, then restore; confirm audio continues and the same video/position is retained. Pause deliberately before minimizing and confirm restoring leaves it paused.
7. Let Windows sleep and wake; confirm playback stays paused and Play works again. Repeat while minimized.
8. Disconnect and try Load, reconnect, then Retry. Confirm it reloads the submitted link and requires Play.
9. Close and reopen. Confirm preferences restore and playback does not start automatically.

The GitHub Windows workflow builds the installer/portable downloads after these automated checks. It does not replace the live streaming acceptance above.

The requested background-player feature is restricted by YouTube's API developer policies (III.I.9). See the README for this change from the original visible-player-only design; automated correctness checks do not establish API policy compliance.
