# Listening room artwork

Project asset: `public/art/night-room.webp`.

Created with the built-in image-generation tool using the user-supplied player image as a style reference. The generated image was encoded as WebP without changing its composition. The animated vinyl, tonearm, and lamp glow are separate app layers; controls and the YouTube video are not painted into the image.

Prompt:

> A standalone landscape pixel-art background for Haru.fm, approximately 3:2. Only the room illustration: no app window, borders, controls, song title, text, or logos. A dark midnight navy study room, a warm amber desk lamp at upper left, plant and stacked books, and a nighttime apartment skyline and crescent moon through a window at upper right. A dark wooden desk in the foreground, with a charcoal turntable in the center and an open lid. A clean empty black platter near 54% across and 74% down, unobstructed and without a label or tonearm, for separate animated layers. Coffee to the left, notebook and pencil to the right. Crisp pixel art and limited dithering, subdued slate/navy with warm amber light, matching the supplied late-night listening mood. No characters or monitor: the app supplies a real CRT-framed YouTube screen separately. Not a screenshot.

App icon: `public/favicon.svg`, with PNG and ICO equivalents. It is a simple app-native record symbol using the same navy and amber palette.
